Important classes:
========================
- page-header – Tabler page heading layout.
- page-title – Heading styling.
- row – Grid layout.
- align-items-center – Vertically aligns elements.
- col-auto – Takes only the required width.
- ms-auto – Pushes the button to the right.


Important modal attributes:
==========================
Attribute	Purpose
data-bs-toggle="modal"	Opens modal
data-bs-target="#userModal"	Identifies which modal to open
data-bs-dismiss="modal"	Closes modal
modal-dialog	Popup positioning
modal-content	Modal content wrapper
modal-header	Modal heading
modal-body	Main content
modal-footer	Footer buttons
